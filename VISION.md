# Vision: Arm Decision Proof v1

`BlueprintCapturePipeline` compiles the maintained Site-Task Testbed, executes
the bounded Task Evaluation Run, seals the prospective result, and joins the
held-out physical outcomes.

The shared block below is the complete active vision. Broader historical visions
remain in git history and do not authorize work.

## Vision

### The Only Active Horizon

Blueprint's current horizon is **one prospectively physically validated
site-specific arm decision**.

The company must show that a robot team can give Blueprint one real fixed-arm
workcell, task, and two runnable candidates; Blueprint can construct the minimum
replica needed to make a sealed selection, elimination, or abstention; and a
held-out physical trial can adjudicate the same practical decision and a
predicted failure boundary.

Until that is observed, broader visions are not roadmaps.

### Immediate Sequence

Blueprint has qualified the decision/outcome harness retrospectively on SIMPLER.
It next qualifies the scene-construction service on exact public data: metric
InteriorGS/matching-SAGE-3D appearance/collision pairing, one source-object
removal, unchanged Inpaint360GS reproduction, InFusion primary completion and
AuraFusion360 challenge using explicitly render-derived method inputs, one exact
SimReady USD replacement, a manual-versus-NVIDIA Content Agents authoring check,
one targeted ScanNet++ measured transfer, deterministic variation, abstention,
and full simulator-side replay.

Only after that public matrix and partner protocol pass does Blueprint take one
fresh capture of one previously unseen workcell, including registered
clean-background and object-present observations. Fresh capture is the final new
construction input; two-candidate execution, sealing, held-out physical trials,
and adjudication follow it.

This sequence is not open-ended capture or 3DGS research. New features or methods
are added only when a gate identifies a specific missing measurement and an exact
released implementation. The durable value under test is the evidence-bounded
Task Evaluation Run, including a reversible splat-to-SimReady replacement seam,
not ownership of reconstruction or inpainting.

### What This Proof Would Establish

A successful first proof would establish only that, for the exact partner, site,
task, robot, policies, condition distribution, and validation envelope:

- the candidate replica supported a useful prospective testing decision;
- the evidence chain was reproducible and inspectable;
- one failure boundary was localized usefully;
- the partner changed or reduced its next physical-test allocation;
- the testbed has a credible reuse path.

It would not establish universal policy ranking, deployment readiness, safety,
cross-site or cross-embodiment transfer, humanoid readiness, or a general digital
twin.

### What Failure Would Teach

- If the decision is wrong, retain the contradiction and identify whether the
  smallest missing factor was observation, geometry, dynamics, latency, reset,
  task distribution, policy integration, or statistics.
- If the system abstains correctly, the evidence boundary works, but the
  showcase remains incomplete.
- If direct physical testing is cheaper or the partner will not reuse the
  replica, the economic thesis is unsupported even if the software works.
- If manual authoring dominates, Blueprint has not found a scalable minimum
  replica.
- If no measurable missing input explains repeated inconclusive results, stop
  the thesis rather than adding infrastructure.

### Conditional Future

Only after the first proof and partner-value verdict may Blueprint choose a new
goal. Candidate decisions include repeating the same method on a second
fixed-arm site-task, offering bounded capture/testbed preparation, investigating
one measured fidelity blocker, or stopping.

Humanoids, broader manipulation, multi-site generalization, learned/world-model
evaluation, post-training, marketplaces, standards, and deployment are
uncommitted options. None is automatically next, and none authorizes present
work.

### Invariants

1. Raw capture and authoritative physical outcomes outrank derived artifacts.
2. The product is a Task Evaluation Run, not a replica or backend.
3. Backends remain swappable behind stable contracts.
4. Decisions are bounded estimates or abstentions, never guarantees.
5. Rights, privacy, provenance, holdout integrity, and physical safety remain
   human-authorized and fail closed.
6. A failed or contradictory result is immutable evidence, not a marketing
   success.
7. Every task must directly remove an Arm Decision Proof gate blocker.
