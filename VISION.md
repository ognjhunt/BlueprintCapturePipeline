# Vision: Free Beta and Arm Decision Proof v1

`BlueprintCapturePipeline` builds the simulation of the site for the job
packet, runs the early trial as bounded Task Evaluation Runs, and produces Arm
Decision Proof's sealed prospective result joined to held-out physical
outcomes.

The shared block below is the complete active vision. Broader historical visions
remain in git history and do not authorize work.

## Vision

### Active Horizons

Blueprint has two current horizons, one for each active program.

The **free beta** horizon is **real jobs turned into job packets that
deployment companies answer**. A site films one recurring job once. Blueprint
turns the video into a job packet: the site's own sentence for the job, the
video with its rights recorded, a first look on paper, a simulation of the
site with the job's object in it, the success rule, and, where one exists, an
early trial of openly available robot software. With the site's permission
Blueprint sends it to deployment companies it has approved and shows the site
their responses. The site and a deployment company agree any work directly.

The service accepts different sites and tasks through the same durable stores,
queues and capability contracts. Supported profiles accept new site data without
code edits; a new task family may need real runtime, reset, scorer and asset
engineering. Broad intake and recoverable routing do not establish universal
robot competence or cross-site physical validation.

The **Arm Decision Proof** horizon is **one prospectively physically validated
site-specific arm decision**. The company must show that a robot team can give Blueprint one real fixed-arm
workcell, task, and two runnable candidates; Blueprint can construct the minimum
replica needed to make a sealed selection, elimination, or abstention; and a
held-out physical trial can adjudicate the same practical decision and a
predicted failure boundary.

Until these are observed, broader visions are not roadmaps.

### Arm Decision Proof Sequence

Blueprint has qualified the decision/outcome harness retrospectively on SIMPLER.
The historical public-scene construction plan used metric
InteriorGS/matching-SAGE-3D appearance/collision pairing, one source-object
removal, unchanged Inpaint360GS reproduction, InFusion primary completion and
AuraFusion360 challenge using explicitly render-derived method inputs, one exact
SimReady USD replacement, a manual-versus-NVIDIA Content Agents authoring check,
one targeted ScanNet++ measured transfer, deterministic variation, abstention,
and full simulator-side replay. The owner-selected appearance path is now
artifixer3D+ with `gpt-image-2`; Inpaint360GS and AuraFusion360 remain retired
execution lanes, and fresh Raw V3.2 capture replaces ScanNet++ as the required
real metric transfer. Follow the current ADP lane-reachability record, preserve
its historical `7/10` suite, and do not rewrite frozen proof definitions.

Only after that public matrix and partner protocol pass does Arm Decision Proof
take its one fresh capture of one previously unseen workcell, including
registered clean-background and object-present observations. That capture is
the proof's final new construction input; two-candidate execution, sealing,
held-out physical trials, and adjudication follow it. This sequence gates only
the proof's capture. Free beta site captures are not held behind it.

This sequence is not open-ended capture or 3DGS research. New features or methods
are added only when a gate identifies a specific missing measurement and an exact
released implementation. The durable value under test is the evidence-bounded
Task Evaluation Run, including a reversible splat-to-SimReady replacement seam,
not ownership of reconstruction or inpainting.

### What This Proof Would Establish

A successful first proof would establish only that, for the exact partner, site,
task, robot, policies, condition distribution, and validation envelope:

- the candidate replica supported a useful prospective testing decision;
- the evidence chain was reproducible and inspectable;
- one failure boundary was localized usefully;
- the partner changed or reduced its next physical-test allocation;
- the testbed has a credible reuse path.

It would not establish universal policy ranking, deployment readiness, safety,
cross-site or cross-embodiment transfer, humanoid readiness, or a general digital
twin.

### What Failure Would Teach

- If the decision is wrong, retain the contradiction and identify whether the
  smallest missing factor was observation, geometry, dynamics, latency, reset,
  task distribution, policy integration, or statistics.
- If the system abstains correctly, the evidence boundary works, but the
  showcase remains incomplete.
- If direct physical testing is cheaper or the partner will not reuse the
  replica, the economic thesis is unsupported even if the software works.
- If manual authoring dominates, Blueprint has not found a scalable minimum
  replica.
- If no measurable missing input explains repeated inconclusive results, stop
  the thesis rather than adding infrastructure.

### Conditional Future

Only after the first proof and partner-value verdict may Blueprint choose the
next proof goal. Candidate decisions include repeating the same method on a
second fixed-arm site-task, offering bounded capture/testbed preparation, investigating
one measured fidelity blocker, or stopping.

Humanoids, broader manipulation, cross-site generalization claims,
learned/world-model evaluation, post-training, provider marketplaces, standards,
and new deployment envelopes are uncommitted options. None is automatically
next, and none authorizes present work. The free beta's job-packet journey is
active work.

Blueprint's long-term vision is the independent proving ground where every job
is tested against every robot: a site posts once and is told the moment a robot
can do its job, and the backlog of jobs is a live map of real demand. It is a
direction, not current work, and it authorizes no present work.

### Invariants

1. Raw capture and authoritative physical outcomes outrank derived artifacts.
2. The packet factory starts from retained video and builds a job packet.
   Task Evaluation Runs are bounded evidence, and neither
   fixture tests nor queued requests prove actual policy execution.
3. Backends remain swappable behind stable contracts.
4. Decisions are bounded estimates or abstentions, never guarantees.
5. Rights, privacy, provenance, holdout integrity, and physical safety remain
   human-authorized and fail closed.
6. A failed or contradictory result is immutable evidence, not a marketing
   success.
7. Every task must directly remove a blocker in an active program: a free beta
   job-packet step or an Arm Decision Proof gate.
