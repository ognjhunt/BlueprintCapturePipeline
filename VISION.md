# Vision: Free Beta

`BlueprintCapturePipeline` builds the simulation of the site for the job
packet and runs the early trial as bounded Task Evaluation Runs.

The shared block below is the complete active vision. Broader historical visions
remain in git history and do not authorize work.

## Vision

### Active Horizons

Blueprint has one current horizon.

The **free beta** horizon is **real jobs turned into job packets that
deployment companies answer**. A site films one recurring job once. Blueprint
turns the video into a job packet: the site's own sentence for the job, the
video with its rights recorded, a first look on paper, a simulation of the
site with the job's object in it, the success rule, and, where one exists, an
early trial of openly available robot software. Sharing is the service:
Blueprint shares it with the deployment companies it has approved and shows
the site their responses. The site and a deployment company agree any work directly.

The service accepts different sites and tasks through the same durable stores,
queues and capability contracts. Supported profiles accept new site data without
code edits; a new task family may need real runtime, reset, scorer and asset
engineering. Broad intake and recoverable routing do not establish universal
robot competence or cross-site physical validation.

Until that is observed, broader visions are not roadmaps.

### Conditional Future

Broader manipulation claims, cross-site generalization claims,
learned/world-model evaluation, post-training, provider marketplaces, standards,
and new deployment envelopes are uncommitted options. None is automatically
next, and none authorizes present work. The free beta's job-packet journey is
active work.

Blueprint's long-term vision is the independent proving ground where every job
is tested against every robot: a site posts once and is told the moment a robot
can do its job, and the backlog of jobs is a live map of real demand. It is a
direction, not current work, and it authorizes no present work.

### Long-term business model

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-13 as the model to keep in mind. It is for
later: none of it is built, priced or promised now, and it authorizes no
present work.

The end goal, in the owner's words: the "linkedin for robot jobs/deployment
opportunities", with a fleet management layer under it: how robots get to and
from each job, along with payments and contracts.

At that end state the money comes from the work, not from the introduction.
Four layers:

1. **Payments and contracts.** Work is contracted and paid through Blueprint,
   and Blueprint keeps a percentage of what is paid. The success rule in each
   job packet (what counts as the job done) is the measure the work is paid on.
2. **Fleet.** Whoever operates the robots pays a subscription for each robot.
3. **The network.** Profiles are free for sites and for deployment companies.
   Large buyers pay for hiring tools and for the map of demand.
4. **Testing.** Robot makers pay to have their robots tested against real
   jobs. A company pays for the test, never for the result or for its rank.

The software gets Blueprint into the transaction; the payments are the
business. A site pays nothing to post a job or to get its answer.

What is true today does not change because of this. Deployment companies will
pay; how is not decided, and no price is stated anywhere. Each introduction is
recorded as a billable event and nothing is charged. Blueprint charges no fee
for and takes no percentage of any pilot or deployment, and its Terms say so
until the owner replaces that sentence. Whether introductions are charged
during the beta is the owner's decision and has not been made.

### Invariants

1. Raw capture and authoritative physical outcomes outrank derived artifacts.
2. The packet factory starts from retained video and builds a job packet.
   Task Evaluation Runs are bounded evidence, and neither
   fixture tests nor queued requests prove actual policy execution.
3. Backends remain swappable behind stable contracts.
4. Decisions are bounded estimates or abstentions, never guarantees.
5. Rights, privacy, provenance, holdout integrity, and physical safety remain
   human-authorized and fail closed.
6. A failed or contradictory result is immutable evidence, not a marketing
   success.
7. Every task must directly remove a blocker in a free beta job-packet step.
