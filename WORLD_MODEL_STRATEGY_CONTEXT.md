## Strategic Doctrine

### Sole Strategy

All backend and infrastructure choices serve **Arm Decision Proof v1**. The
strategy is not to build a world model, simulator, reconstruction company,
provider marketplace, or general benchmark. It is to make one prospective,
site-specific fixed-arm decision and physically adjudicate it.

World models, classical simulators, reconstruction systems, 3DGS renderers,
SimReady tools, analytic geometry, learned evaluators, and physical evidence are
replaceable evidence methods behind the existing Task Evaluation Run contracts.
No method gains authority from novelty, realism, provider identity, or a paper's
reported result.

### Public Harness First, Partner Stack Second

First audit and pin one existing public real-to-sim substrate. SIMPLER is the
provisional first candidate because it exposes prepackaged manipulation
environments, common policy integrations, condition variation, and public real
performance references. Its admission still requires exact repository,
submodule, asset, license, policy, task, evaluator, outcome, and runtime pins.

Use it to qualify the complete harness retrospectively. Do not integrate
PolaRiS, REALM, DROID, or another backend in parallel unless SIMPLER fails a
recorded requirement. Do not select a permanent robot, simulator, policy family,
renderer, provider, or reconstruction system from this development result.

For the later prospective proof, partner first and backend second:

After admission:

1. record the partner's working robot, observation/action interface, policy
   runtime, simulator, source assets, and task;
2. identify the exact missing evidence for the bounded decision;
3. reuse the partner's and Blueprint's existing stack where it meets the need;
4. add only the thinnest adapter or measurement required;
5. qualify each method for its exact claim and envelope;
6. abstain when the evidence is insufficient.

A DROID/Franka partner may justify one stack; another fixed arm may justify
another. That does not change the product or proof contract.

### Development Substrates

Existing captures and scenes are the default way to make the downstream harness
runnable before new capture arrives. Pin the smallest corpus and its digests.
Use it for deterministic compiler, matrix, reset, runtime, receipt, replay,
sealing, outcome-join, aggregation, abstention, and presentation tests.

All reused assets retain `development_only` status. They cannot validate the new
capture, site-task sufficiency, physical dynamics, policy-domain match,
sim-to-real decision fidelity, or customer value. Fixture success is a software
result, not a scientific or commercial result.

Until the one-command public-reference harness is complete, nearly all
engineering effort goes to external source ingestion, normalized contracts,
two-candidate execution, condition receipts, replay, decision sealing, external
outcome joins, statistics, abstention, and evidence presentation. New capture or
reconstruction feature work is frozen unless a measured blocker proves it is the
smallest missing measurement.

### Minimum Replica Rule

Build only what the policy observation and task decision require:

- captured appearance only where the policy observes it;
- independently qualified simplified collision geometry for the robot, work
  surface, tray, fixture, and rigid objects;
- measured coordinate frames and scale;
- owner-approved resets, conditions, and metrics;
- matched control rate, latency model, and interfaces where decision-relevant.

Do not model the whole facility. Do not add fidelity without a measured failure
or preregistered requirement. The candidate MSER becomes sufficient only if the
physical holdout supports the sealed practical decision inside the stated
envelope.

### Stable Contracts

Keep these stable across backend swaps:

- raw capture bundle, native timelines, and provenance;
- site, task, robot, camera, object, and coordinate-frame identities;
- maintained Site-Task Testbed identity, version, and digest;
- Decision/Evidence Request and qualified Evidence Plan;
- `EvaluationRunSpec` seams: `scene_bundle`, `robot_adapter`,
  `task_scenario_pack`, `policy_adapter`, `runtime_provider_profile`, and
  `proof_contract`;
- condition IDs, reset/evaluator contracts, episode receipts, and replay;
- sealed decision, holdout partition/release, normalized result, Decision
  Envelope, and Physical Outcome Join;
- rights, privacy, authorization, audit, spend, watchdog, and teardown contracts;
- explicit compatibility readers and translators.

### Scientific Boundary

External research motivates candidates; it does not qualify Blueprint results.
Simulation and generated evidence remains inside its measured validation
envelope. General rank correlation requires enough independent policies and is
not a claim of the two-candidate first proof.

Before physical holdout release, freeze candidates, task distribution,
conditions, resets, success rules, invalid-trial handling, minimum meaningful
difference, power analysis, statistical method, stop rule, decision rule, code,
assets, dependencies, and seeds. Seal the simulated result. Amendments create a
new version.

Candidate policies and agents cannot see holdouts, evaluator internals,
privileged state, or proof authority. Failed and invalid cells remain in the
ledger. Physical learning occurs only through authoritative exact-ID joins.

### Build Admission

A new backend, provider, model, engine, reconstruction method, or paid resource
is admitted only when:

1. an observed active-program blocker names the missing measurement;
2. existing infrastructure cannot provide it;
3. the proposed method is the smallest credible path;
4. rights, privacy, cost, reproducibility, and exportability are acceptable;
5. qualification and failure tests are defined before execution;
6. any paid work uses the canonical fail-closed allocator, spend cap, watchdog,
   teardown, and provider-zero proof;
7. the adapter remains replaceable.

Provider availability is not qualification. Do not run bakeoffs without a
decision-relevant need.

### Stop Rather Than Expand

Stop or pivot when direct physical testing is cheaper, manual authoring
dominates, reuse is absent, the partner will not use the result, the holdout
contradicts the simulated decision, or repeated inconclusive results reveal no
measurable missing input. More backend sophistication is not the default answer
to a failed business or scientific premise.

World-model, humanoid, dynamic-scene, deformable, universal-runtime,
post-training, multi-site, and provider-marketplace work remains frozen until a
successful Arm Decision Proof and a new explicit strategy decision.
