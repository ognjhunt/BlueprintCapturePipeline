## Strategic Doctrine

### Strategy

All backend and infrastructure choices serve the one active program: the
**free beta's job packet**. The owner direction of 2026-11-12 makes the job
packet the unit of the product. The simulation of
the site is how a job becomes testable without a visit; it is a means, not the
product. Reuse capture, reconstruction and site-package paths to build it.
Blueprint's own test of openly available robot software is an early trial
inside the packet, never the site's answer. It covers every kind of robot
body: arms, mobile manipulators, wheeled humanoids and humanoids. Hardening the
automatic test for every kind of job, several objects or unattended scale is
paused.

World models, classical simulators, reconstruction systems, 3DGS renderers,
SimReady tools, analytic geometry, learned evaluators, and physical evidence are
replaceable evidence methods behind the existing Task Evaluation Run contracts.
No method gains authority from novelty, realism, provider identity, or a paper's
reported result. The Decision/Evidence Router chooses qualified methods for the
question and admitted envelope. Customers need not choose a simulator or
provider. Measurement-catalog templates propose request-specific thresholds;
they do not approve them or establish universal task support.

Use the existing supported licensed independent-execution adapters first.
Approved team-hosted endpoints and integrations remain valid access routes;
team operation is not mandatory. Bind actual robot/tool/sensor/control,
observation/action/preprocessing, policy and runtime identities where needed.
Declarations do not prove tested compatibility or a partner's willingness to
deliver. Do not turn advertised API access, open weights, an application or a
registry label into runnable admission. Preserve controlled observations,
independent scoring, immutable inputs, episode media, and runtime receipts.

Task requirements and scoring, site geometry/objects, embodiment/tool/sensors,
policy interfaces and delivery availability are separate integration seams.
Route by declared capabilities and validated compatibility; do not silently
coerce multi-step, articulated or unsupported work into rigid pick-and-place.
The first exact fixed-arm/DROID-compatible route does not admit every robot,
and a fidelity template is not a runtime adapter. Atlas and unavailable hosted
APIs remain unavailable. Reuse existing intent/outbox/lease and reconciliation
mechanisms for durable at-least-once delivery and restart recovery; an ambiguous
timeout cannot justify another paid run.

Reuse cannot turn correlated prior evidence into independent repeated proof;
reported outcomes cannot recalibrate a method without an authoritative
Physical Outcome Join.

### Development Substrates And Editing

Existing captures and exact public scenes are the default way to make the full
service runnable before fresh capture arrives. Pin the smallest corpus and its
digests. Use it for metric registration, object partition/removal,
render-derived virtual-camera inputs, released-code inpainting, SimReady USD
authoring and insertion, collision/contact/dynamics, deterministic variation,
compiler, matrix, reset, runtime, receipt, replay, sealing, outcome-join,
aggregation, abstention, and presentation tests.

All reused assets retain `development_only` status. They cannot validate the
fresh capture, site-task sufficiency, factual recovery of never-observed hidden
surfaces, real physical dynamics, policy-domain match, sim-to-real decision
fidelity, or customer value. Fixture success is a software result, not a
scientific or commercial result.

If the released inpainting path retrains an editable splat from render-derived
views, record that new artifact identity. Do not call it an in-place edit of the
publisher PLY unless exact initialization from that PLY is separately proven.

New capture feature work is frozen unless a measured blocker proves it is the
smallest missing measurement.

### Minimum Replica Rule

Build only what the policy observation and task decision require:

- captured appearance only where the policy observes it;
- registered 3DGS or explicit visual geometry for appearance, never measurement
  or collision authority;
- independently qualified simplified static collision geometry for the work
  surface, tray, and fixture;
- one exact SimReady USD per task object, with separate visual/collision meshes,
  measured dimensions/pose, mass, center of mass, inertia, friction, restitution,
  semantic identity, and reset;
- measured coordinate frames and scale;
- owner-approved resets, conditions, and metrics;
- matched control rate, latency model, and interfaces where decision-relevant.

Do not model the whole facility. Remove a source object from both appearance and
collision before inserting its replacement; a visual edit may never leave a
ghost collider. Prefer observed clean-background/source-view evidence.
Generated completion remains `visual_candidate_only`. Do not add fidelity
without a measured failure or preregistered requirement. The candidate MSER
becomes sufficient only if the physical holdout supports the sealed practical
decision inside the stated envelope.

NVIDIA USD Content Agents is a replaceable conversion, material, physics-schema,
and static-validation backend after source geometry exists. It is not an
image-to-CAD system. Agent-authored CAD is admissible only as immutable source
code, parameters, kernel, and output that pass independent metric, collider,
physics, and dynamic Isaac gates.

### Stable Contracts

Keep these stable across backend swaps:

- raw capture bundle, native timelines, and provenance;
- site, task, robot, camera, object, and coordinate-frame identities;
- maintained Site-Task Testbed identity, version, and digest;
- Decision/Evidence Request and qualified Evidence Plan;
- `EvaluationRunSpec` seams: `scene_bundle`, `robot_adapter`,
  `task_scenario_pack`, `policy_adapter`, `runtime_provider_profile`, and
  `proof_contract`;
- condition IDs, reset/evaluator contracts, episode receipts, and replay;
- sealed decision, holdout partition/release, normalized result, Decision
  Envelope, and Physical Outcome Join;
- rights, privacy, authorization, audit, spend, watchdog, and teardown contracts;
- explicit compatibility readers and translators.

### Scientific Boundary

External research motivates candidates; it does not qualify Blueprint results.
Simulation and generated evidence remains inside its measured validation
envelope. General rank correlation requires enough independent policies.

Before physical holdout release, freeze candidates, task distribution,
conditions, resets, success rules, invalid-trial handling, minimum meaningful
difference, power analysis, statistical method, stop rule, decision rule, code,
assets, dependencies, and seeds. Seal the simulated result. Amendments create a
new version.

Candidate policies and agents cannot see holdouts, evaluator internals,
privileged state, or proof authority. Failed and invalid cells remain in the
ledger. Physical learning occurs only through authoritative exact-ID joins.

### Build Admission

A new backend, provider, model, engine, reconstruction method, or paid resource
is admitted only when:

1. an observed active-program blocker names the missing measurement;
2. existing infrastructure cannot provide it;
3. the proposed method is the smallest credible path;
4. rights, privacy, cost, reproducibility, and exportability are acceptable;
5. qualification and failure tests are defined before execution;
6. any paid work uses the canonical fail-closed allocator, spend cap, watchdog,
   teardown, and provider-zero proof;
7. the adapter remains replaceable.

Provider availability is not qualification. Do not run bakeoffs without a
decision-relevant need.

### Stop Rather Than Expand

Stop or pivot when direct physical testing is cheaper, manual authoring
dominates, reuse is absent, the partner will not use the result, the holdout
contradicts the simulated decision, or repeated inconclusive results reveal no
measurable missing input. More backend sophistication is not the default answer
to a failed business or scientific premise.

World-model, dynamic-scene, deformable, universal-runtime, post-training,
cross-site generalization, and provider-marketplace work remains frozen unless
an observed active-program blocker and explicit scope decision require the
smallest dependency. Building another task family still needs the recorded
owner focus decision based on demand evidence.
Operating several free beta jobs is not a cross-site generalization claim.
