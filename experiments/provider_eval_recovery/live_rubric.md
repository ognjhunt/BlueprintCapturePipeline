# Private comparison protocol, recovery revision 1

This protocol is separate from the Library reviewer specification, which remains
parent-side. It cannot replace that specification or its reviewed oracle.

Keep controller/retrieval inputs limited to the frozen public cases. Compare
Parallel Fast/Advanced and Perplexity Fast/standard with identical question text,
one search per cell, ten normalized sources, and 12,000 evidence characters.
Use only gpt-6.1-sol, Standard billing, at most 6,000 counted input tokens and
2,048 output tokens including reasoning. No automatic retry on uncertain calls.

Retain citations, dated provenance, exact product/version, bounded task, evidence
class, availability/geography, limits/conflicts and explicit remaining unknowns.
Separate vendor claims, research demonstrations, named pilots/deployments and
roadmaps. Do not transfer evidence across generations, treat specifications as
task demonstrations, or qualify hypothetical sites. Never include private CRM.

The isolated parent-side reviewer must judge source entailment, source primacy,
dated citation support, unsupported assertions, conflicts and appropriate unknowns
using its frozen oracle. Unknowns remain in the denominator. Structural citation
checks alone cannot establish factual accuracy or a winner. Report raw retrieval
quality separately from the identical Sol synthesis and retain every latency and
cost component, including counting, inference, cache writes and uncertain charges.
