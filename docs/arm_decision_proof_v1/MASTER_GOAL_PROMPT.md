# Starting Goal Prompt — Public-Reference Arm Decision Harness

Use this as the first autonomous implementation goal for Arm Decision Proof v1.
Its terminal is **ADP-008**, the complete retrospective public-reference harness.
It deliberately does not authorize the later partner/physical-proof phase.

```text
/goal

Act as Blueprint's autonomous execution lead for the first phase of its sole
active program, ARM DECISION PROOF V1 (`arm-decision-proof-v1`).

YOUR ONE GOAL

Complete ADP-001 through ADP-008: prove that Blueprint can take one exactly
pinned public real-to-sim manipulation substrate, run exactly two genuine frozen
policy candidates through a normalized evaluation harness, seal a development
decision before programmatic release of published physical-reference outcomes,
join those outcomes exactly, and reproduce the full bounded evidence package
with one command.

Do not build new capture or reconstruction features. Treat capture, CAD,
calibration, meshes, simulator scenes, and other site representations as
replaceable inputs. Only reopen capture/reconstruction work if an observed,
recorded harness blocker proves that one specific missing measurement is the
smallest path to ADP-008.

Do not stop after writing a plan, architecture note, schema, or fixture. After a
short evidence-based audit, implement and verify the smallest unfinished
critical-path slice. Continue until ADP-008 passes or a real external, rights,
compute, or human-authority blocker prevents further safe work.

WHY THIS GOAL EXISTS

The corrected Blueprint sequence is:

existing public scene + real policies + published physical results
-> qualify the complete Blueprint harness retrospectively
-> substitute one future partner's existing site/task/assets
-> collect new capture only for measured missing evidence
-> seal a genuinely prospective decision
-> adjudicate it with a randomized physical holdout.

This goal covers only the first two lines. The public result is software and
evidence-chain qualification, not the final scientific or customer proof.

NORTH STAR THAT THIS PHASE SERVES

From one qualified, rights-cleared representation of one previously unseen
fixed-arm workcell, imported from an existing scene or capture when possible and
newly captured only for measured gaps, prospectively decide which of two frozen
policy or configuration candidates deserves the next scarce physical-test
budget, or explicitly abstain, then verify the decision and at least one
predicted failure boundary with randomized held-out physical trials.

North-star metric:
`prospectively_physically_validated_new_site_task_decisions`
Current = 0. Target = 1.

ADP-008 does not increment this metric. It makes the machinery credible enough
to begin the prospective partner proof.

PRODUCT AND CLAIM BOUNDARIES

- Product: one bounded Task Evaluation Run.
- Reusable substrate: one maintained Site-Task Testbed.
- Construction method: candidate Minimum Sufficient Evaluation Replica.
- Scientific adjudication: Physical Outcome Join.
- Optional showcase label: SiteBench.
- Immediate phase label: `retrospective_external_reference` and
  `development_only`.
- Never claim digital twin, prospective validation, deployment readiness,
  safety, customer value, general sim-to-real fidelity, general policy ranking,
  or universal robot/simulator support from this phase.
- Exactly two candidates can support one bounded selection/elimination decision;
  they cannot establish rank correlation.

EXECUTION ORDER

Work one critical path, in order. Do not create parallel product lanes.

PHASE 0 — Establish truth

- Validate ADP-001 focus/schema/doctrine tests.
- Produce a concise current-state table for ADP-002..ADP-008 with only
  `observed_complete`, `partial`, or `missing` statuses and evidence links.
- Identify the smallest unfinished item. Begin implementing it immediately.

PHASE 1 — ADP-002 public-reference admission

- Research primary sources and official repositories.
- Emit a machine-valid immutable source/admission manifest using existing schema
  conventions where possible.
- Record licenses/rights, exact identities, hashes, control/evaluator semantics,
  runtime requirements, physical-outcome granularity, and claim ceiling.
- Add deterministic validation and fail-closed tests.
- Stop with a compact admission-failure packet if no source can meet the exact
  task/candidate/outcome join contract without invention.

PHASE 2 — ADP-003 generic two-candidate execution

- Drive candidate count from the run request.
- Preserve five-policy compatibility.
- Reject fake padding, duplicate candidate identities, mutable policy identities,
  and unbound checkpoints.
- Prove two genuine candidates generate stable candidate/condition IDs.

PHASE 3 — ADP-004 receipts and replay

- Wire condition -> reset -> policy query -> simulator steps -> independent
  metric -> validity -> immutable episode receipt.
- The candidate cannot grade itself or access hidden evaluator state.
- Bind source, environment, task, candidate, condition, seed, code, dependencies,
  observations/actions, resets, outcomes, failures, and artifact digests.
- Replay must reproduce the normalized receipt or emit a typed
  non-reproducibility failure.

PHASE 4 — ADP-005 decision sealing and outcome firebreak

- Freeze calibration/development partitions.
- Make public physical-reference outcomes programmatically inaccessible to the
  execution and decision code until after the decision seal exists.
- Bind candidate, task, matrix, metric, rule, source, code, and artifact digests
  into the seal.
- Emit a separate release/custodian receipt.
- Reject early access, changed inputs, late thresholds, silent amendments, and
  a join against a mismatched seal.
- State explicitly that this software firebreak does not make already published
  outcomes a genuinely unseen scientific holdout.

PHASE 5 — ADP-006 bounded decision rule

- Reuse or implement the smallest deterministic compiler for baseline, minimum
  decision-relevant difference, alpha, power, design, invalid-trial handling,
  multiplicity, stop rule, and uncertainty.
- Reject arbitrary trial counts and unsupported inference.
- Output only select, eliminate, equivalent/inconclusive, or abstain, plus
  coverage, uncertainty, invalid region, contradiction, and next cheapest
  missing measurement.

PHASE 6 — ADP-007 evidence matrix

- Render every candidate-by-condition cell, including failures and invalids.
- Link source, environment, reset, execution receipt, trace/media if present,
  metric, physical-reference outcome, versions, digests, and qualification.
- Keep missing outcomes visibly missing.
- Display `retrospective_external_reference` and `development_only` prominently.

PHASE 7 — ADP-008 integrated replay

- Run one admitted task and exactly two admitted policies through the full path.
- Seal before release, join exactly, preserve disagreement, and render the
  artifact index/evidence matrix.
- Provide one documented command that another engineer can run from immutable
  inputs.
- Run the smallest deterministic verification set that covers every changed
  contract and security/evidence boundary.

EVIDENCE INTEGRITY RULES

- Observed evidence only. Agents may propose; deterministic digest-bound code
  qualifies, authorizes, joins, decides, or abstains.
- Do not hand-author fake JSON to make a gate pass. Test fixtures may exercise
  failure paths but may not qualify the integrated run.
- Physical outcomes remain externally sourced, immutable, and authoritative.
- A policy never owns task truth, reset truth, evaluation, hidden conditions,
  decision rules, or outcome release.
- Failed, invalid, timed-out, interrupted, and missing trials remain in the
  ledger and aggregation denominator according to the frozen rule.
- Never convert aggregate published performance into trial-level evidence or
  false condition matches.
- Every amendment creates a new version; no historical seal or verdict changes.
- A contradiction is a result, not a reason to rewrite the experiment.
- Abstention is correct system behavior when evidence is insufficient.

DEFINITION OF DONE FOR THIS GOAL

ADP-008 is complete only when observed artifacts prove all of the following:

1. One public substrate is admitted by exact revision, asset, license, task,
   candidate, physical-reference, runtime, and claim-ceiling bindings.
2. Exactly two genuine frozen candidates execute closed loop through the same
   normalized interface.
3. Every planned condition produces a complete receipt or retained typed
   failure/invalid record.
4. Replay is deterministic or fails explicitly as non-reproducible.
5. The development decision is sealed before the programmatic outcome release.
6. The join rejects mismatched task, candidate, condition, version, or seal IDs.
7. The bounded verdict exposes agreement, contradiction, uncertainty, coverage,
   invalid region, and abstention correctly.
8. The evidence matrix links every claim to its source artifacts and digests.
9. One command reproduces the admitted result from immutable inputs or provides
   exact acquisition steps for immutable cached inputs.
10. The result is visibly retrospective and development-only.
11. No new capture/reconstruction feature was added without a recorded measured
    blocker.
12. Focus/schema, changed-contract, outcome-firebreak, compatibility, and replay
    tests pass under the risk-based test policy.

Not done: a plan, manifest alone, mocked candidates, fixture-only vertical slice,
successful simulator launch, aggregate score table, generated report without
receipts, or an unsealed join.
```
