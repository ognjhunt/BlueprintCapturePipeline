## Decision

1. AuraFusion360 is the selected ADP-v1 public-scene editing path. The selected
   execution is `live_v12_openclip_execute` on exact InteriorGS scene `840313`,
   target `ins160`, using the frozen eight-camera render-derived packet. The
   result remains a visual candidate rather than hidden-background truth or an
   in-place edit of the publisher PLY.
2. Inpaint360GS and InFusion remain exact, documented, nonblocking typed
   abstentions. Their receipts and failed or blocked executions are preserved.
   This decision does not manufacture missing publisher license authority.
3. ScanNet++ is no longer a prerequisite for the next gate. One fresh,
   rights-cleared Raw V3.2 workcell capture must instead supply the real metric
   transfer, including object-present and clean-background observations,
   calibrated poses/intrinsics, gravity/up, depth or mesh, scale, and the
   preregistered physical measurements. This substitution moves the evidence;
   it does not waive it.
4. The historical ten-role `public_scene_suite_index.v1` remains blocked at
   `7/10`. It is never rewritten or described as complete. The three retained
   blocked roles remain Inpaint360GS author smoke, InFusion primary adapter,
   and ScanNet++ real transfer.
5. The bounded public-scene construction rehearsal is accepted. Real
   metrology, policy validity, sim-to-real fidelity, and physical outcomes move
   to the fresh-site partner proof.

## What is accepted

The evidence is sufficient to continue from the public construction rehearsal
because it demonstrates, on one rights-admitted public scene, that Blueprint
can:

- bind an appearance scene to its exact static-collision companion;
- select a rigid target from frozen task-relevant criteria;
- execute released-code background editing on frozen scene-derived cameras;
- deactivate the matching source collider without deleting the support;
- place one exact visual/collision/physics-authored replacement in the shared
  frame;
- run standalone and hybrid static/dynamic Isaac checks; and
- retain digest-bound inputs, outputs, human review, failures, spend, teardown,
  and replay evidence.

## What is not accepted

This decision does not claim:

- ADP-009A ten-role completion;
- factual recovery of the hidden InteriorGS background;
- preservation of the original publisher PLY as Aura's edited representation;
- measurement-authoritative local surface truth from InteriorGS or SAGE;
- a qualified Franka task, learned-policy result, policy ranking, or Cosmos
  policy result;
- fresh-site registration, real policy observation-domain match, sim-to-real
  fidelity, physical safety, or physical task success.

## Required next public-data rehearsal

on the exact sealed Aura/SAGE/SimReady scene. Freeze exactly two runnable learned
policy candidates after source, checkpoint, rights, observation/action, and

action-policy release may enter as one of the two only after its action contract

auxiliary and never becomes an automatic third candidate.

Every completed episode must retain the lossless images actually shown to the
policy, camera/intrinsics/extrinsics, proprioception, instruction, raw action,
executed action, simulator state, contacts, deterministic non-policy success
trace, stdout/stderr, runtime identity, hashes, review video, and provider
teardown proof. This rehearsal remains `development_only` and cannot qualify
the later fresh-site or physical claim.
