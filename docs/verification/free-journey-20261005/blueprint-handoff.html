What changes for people

Site operators still start with one recurring job and authorized capture. The deployed WebApp makes the recording-permission requirement explicit, offers a contact path while permission is arranged, shows that the brief can be corrected, and labels the action “Start free assessment.” Robot teams can apply without an upfront policy upload. The beta evaluation is free to the robot team; the separate site-side $2,500 qualifying-match fee remains unchanged and separately authorized.

Corrections retain the job identity and immutable parent evidence. Unsupported or development evidence remains limited: the rehearsal accepts a signed synthetic failed result, preserves “Development simulation,” and refuses pilot qualification. It does not manufacture a successful robot result.
