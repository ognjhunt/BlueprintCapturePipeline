# Daily discovery, evidence and contact integration

V3 raw discovery retains every sourced operator/site/task hypothesis within the artifact byte envelope. Task and geography evidence are required for a formal raw candidate; robot matching is optional. Without capability evidence, `potential_robot_match` is `unknown`. V1 and v2 discovery contracts retain their earlier limits. V3 findings and candidates have no result-count quota. Artifact, packet, tool, time and authorization boundaries remain enforced.

`discovery_inventory` is an optional raw-output array of distinct discoveries with nullable operator, site, location and task hypothesis, actual source URLs, evidence gap and disposition. Thin, disputed, duplicate, rejected and learning entries remain useful research. An inventory entry cannot become an accepted candidate, CRM promotion or communications task by its disposition alone. Separate sites remain separate records.

The harness writes the complete inventory into immutable 100KB pages and puts a manifest in the packet. Each page has an ordered range, filename, byte length and SHA256. The manifest binds a retained original or repair artifact's actual SHA256 and the typed digest of its complete inventory. Native snapshot and Python export verify the original source, every page, order, count and concatenated inventory digest. A read-only cursor supports resuming page reads without another provider search. An oversized record remains in the original artifact with an explicit size diagnostic; exhausted format repair cannot silently exclude inventory records or claim a complete subset.

New rows pin `blueprint.lead-verification-result.v2`. Historical v1 results keep their original evaluator and exact cohort digest shape. Assessments still use `blueprint.lead-verification.v1`. The canonical field is `version`; an absent canonical field with a matching `schema_version`, or identical aliases, is lossless. Explicit null or conflicting aliases fail. Original assessments, metadata, query records and hashes are preserved. Structured countersearch records are evaluated through their actual query and checked time without rewriting the retained record.

Nested structural diagnostics identify exact version, candidate-digest, timestamp, source-ID/reference, claim and counterevidence paths and feed the existing bounded same-session correction loop. Missing evidence does not cause retries to force qualification. `valid_until: null` truthfully retains unknown freshness and cannot produce promotion or rejection. Supported-looking claims cannot bypass invalid binding or freshness. Positive qualification still requires current retrieved primary support for the named operator, physical site, task and human workflow, a bounded fit inference and counterevidence checks. Buying intent, rights, commercial qualification, robot compatibility and deployment readiness remain separate gates.

Protected publication accepts the same conflict-safe version aliases. A verified raw site/task with an unknown robot match can produce the ordinary CRM row with a blank capability-source URL. The WebApp independently reconstructs that exact row. Unknown capability does not become a capability fact.

Contact gaps return to the existing daily research agent under its original allocation, frozen inputs and tools. Prefer a current relevant professional person, appropriate task team and general business inbox. Complete official operator evidence, current site/task qualification, exact stable CRM identity, suppression, prior history and protected admission govern communications. Truthful contact-null reviewed research creates a research gap and zero paid communications jobs. Every source, admission, run, draft and learning receipt remains separately bound.

Exa Ultra advertises its actual $1 minimum. A smaller requested cap is never raised automatically. The existing authenticated native cap and verified remaining all-in allocation remain required before a provider start; an unavailable allocation produces a truthful skip. Source setup, review and deployment do not require a new numeric research budget, while additional paid execution still requires an actual approved allocation.

A coupled rollout must install the worker package built from the immutable published Pipeline SHA together with its WebApp source changes. Offline tests and package hashes establish source readiness. Production deployment, actual canonical founder Gmail DRAFT/noSENT readback and new matched learning receipts are separate live evidence; sending and automatic first contact remain disabled.
