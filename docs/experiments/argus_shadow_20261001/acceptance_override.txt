BLUEPRINT SHADOW ACCEPTANCE OVERRIDE v1

These instructions override conflicting Argus instructions above. This is a
shadow annotation of a recorded robot policy episode, which may be autonomous
rather than teleoperated. The intended task and the confirmed, digest-bound
task_success_contract are supplied. Do not infer a more convenient goal from
the motion. Name all applicable acceptance criteria before deciding completion.

Evaluate the entire authored contract, including terminal state, destination
containment, orientation, support, release, required settling duration, required
retreat clearance and duration, and authored temporal invariants. A retreat
required by the contract is part of success and completion time. Do not discard
it as parking or incidental motion. A drop followed by recovery, excessive
force, forbidden contact, containment/workspace excursion, retry or regrasp may
fail the whole-episode contract even if the final placement looks correct.
Honor the actual enabled/ignored modes and thresholds; do not invent extra
requirements. Track partial progress separately from contractual success.

Use success only when the supplied evidence supports every required predicate.
Use failure when a required predicate is demonstrably violated. Use unclear
when a required predicate cannot be determined from the supplied evidence,
including occlusion, insufficient temporal sampling, unavailable contact/force
readback, missing state, or uncertain correspondence between video and state.
An occluded final state neither proves failure nor supplies missing confirmation
of a required condition. State/action fields are recorded claims, with units and
time bases, to cross-check against video; never infer forces from pixels.

Preserve goal_reached_at_s and undone_at_s when the full acceptance predicate
was reached and later lost. success_then_undone is a contractual failure for a
task whose success must persist to the end; it does not count as success. Do not
claim the full predicate was reached when only an intermediate subgoal held.
completed_at_s is the first supplied timestamp at which all required predicates
hold and continue to hold as required through termination, including authored
settling/retreat requirements. Set it null for failure, unclear, and
success_then_undone. Report timestamp precision and sampling limitations rather
than presenting interpolated timing as an exact observed event.

Keep the Argus completion fields and temporal annotation schema. Include a
plain-language completion.reason that distinguishes visible facts, native-state
facts, inference, contradictions, and missing evidence. Cite camera IDs and
timestamps, relevant contract criteria, and artifact digests. Retain subtle
failures, transients, recovery, and uncertainty in the timeline. Confidence, if
reported, is annotation confidence; it is not a calibrated probability of
contractual success. Explain disagreements without having access to the current
grader output or the independent review labels.

Argus issue severity describes effects on training data. It is not a physical
safety rating or deployment authorization. Simulator recordings and generative
appearance cannot establish physical validity. This output has no authority to
replace Blueprint scoring, rank policies, promote a claim, or switch an evaluator.
