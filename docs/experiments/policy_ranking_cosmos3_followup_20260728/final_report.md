# Blueprint Cosmos3 successor qualification

Overall verdict: `inconclusive`

The successor produced genuine, structurally valid Cosmos3-Nano videos, but it did not pass the preregistered causal screen. Blueprint therefore abstained before evaluator spend, benchmark-label access, or captured-site claims. This is a terminal result for the frozen experiment, not a claim that all future world-action models will fail.

## cosmos_wam_qualification

Verdict: `inconclusive`

The exact-main runtime loaded pinned `nvidia/Cosmos3-Nano` revision `411f42a8fdfb8c5b2583cb8786e0938f49796eaa` in BF16 on an RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell. It completed two structural canaries and the frozen five-condition by two-seed matrix. All ten scientific responses were first-valid, decodable 17-frame videos, all output hashes were distinct, and the action/request hashes matched the sealed inventory.

The label-blind causal screen found a visual response against the same-seed zero-action video in all 8/8 active rows. That response was not reliably attributable to the supplied action. Only 1/8 rows passed the frozen own-action-versus-strongest-placebo temporal test. Recorded passed at seed 1 but failed seed 0; shuffled, reversed, and policy-swapped failed both seeds. Consequently directional placebo rejection failed, all 0/4 active conditions failed seed robustness, and the screen failed.

The registered independent unit is a DROID session, not a video, seed, view, frame, or control. This arm contains one session and therefore cannot support or falsify general qualification at the declared 95% validity gate. The descriptive failure prevents a powered expansion in this frozen arm and awards no causal credit.

## economics_and_speed

Verdict: `inconclusive`

The three follow-up allocations consumed an estimated `$0.412259` over `1542.915983` provider-observed live seconds. Two infrastructure attempts produced no scientific media. The successful allocation cost an estimated `$0.097141`, loaded Cosmos in `145.029729` seconds, and completed its twelve-request runtime in `184.509450` seconds. The derived post-load time was `39.479721` seconds total, or `3.289977` seconds per generated request on average.

Those figures establish bounded WAM execution economics only. They do not establish useful policy-ranking economics because no evaluator-qualified ranking completed. The exhaustive physical counterfactual lacks an attributable monetary cost and full wall-clock baseline, so “substantially faster and cheaper” is not proven. Follow-up provider charges remain estimates until the invoice settles.

## Overall verdict

`inconclusive`

The frozen stack failed its first causal screen and correctly abstained before generating stronger claims. Because the arm is underpowered for general WAM qualification and never reached ranking, captured-site, or physical-comparison evaluation, the evidence neither supports the thesis nor constitutes a powered falsification of the complete thesis.
