# Beta Data Residency And Transfer Policy

Schema: `blueprint.beta_data_residency_transfer_policy.v1`
Status: `declared_us_only_scope_operator_signoff_required`

The default 100-user beta scope is US testers and US capture sites only. Non-US
participants or non-US capture sites remain blocked until operator evidence
proves signed DPA/SCC or equivalent international-transfer terms, a
transfer-impact assessment, subprocessor residency terms, and regional
processing posture.

The manual operator evidence id is `cross_border_data_residency_posture`.
Launch readiness packets include the JSON policy as
`beta_data_residency_transfer_policy_json`.

This artifact is not signed legal terms, not international-transfer
authorization, and not proof of regional processing.
